# Day 8 Reflection

The most difficult concept for me during this course was understanding JavaScript. Some concepts were challenging to follow, and I still have not fully understood everything. However, I continued learning, practising examples, and asking questions whenever I needed clarification. This helped me understand some concepts better and reminded me that learning programming takes time, patience, and consistent practice.

Another challenge was balancing my course studies with attending the 8 p.m. sessions. On some days, especially when I had been busy or moving around during the day, it was difficult to manage my time and concentrate during the evening sessions. This taught me the importance of planning my time and remaining committed even when my schedule is demanding.

One part of my capstone that I would improve is the working demo. I would make sure all the features work reliably, improve error handling, and test the application on both desktop and mobile screens. I would also practise explaining the API, data model, and architecture more confidently.

Going forward, I want to continue learning JavaScript until I understand it better. I also plan to learn Node.js and Express to build a real backend, followed by SQL and PostgreSQL to store data properly. I understand that I still have more to learn, but I am willing to keep practising and improving.
