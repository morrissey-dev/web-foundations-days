About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple note-taking app that allows you to capture your ideas and thoughts quickly. You can add new notes, view your existing notes, and keep track of your ideas all in one place.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Click the "Add Note" button.
  2. Enter your note in the input field.
  3. Click "Add Note" to save it.

Features

  • Add new notes quickly.
  • View your existing notes.
  • Keep track of your ideas in one place.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + N Create a new note
Ctrl + S Save a note
